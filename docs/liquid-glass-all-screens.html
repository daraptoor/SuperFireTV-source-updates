SuperFire TV · Liquid Glass

Every phone screen, sheet and the player, redesigned with clear Apple‑style glass. All existing elements are kept; only their position, grouping and styling change. Posters, channels and titles are placeholders. The TV interface is not included and stays as it is.

How the new style works

Clear, neutral glass

A light white tint (6–15%) with blur and extra saturation replaces the purple and amber gradients. Content colour shows through, and a 1‑dp bright top edge makes it look like glass.

Floating controls

The header is split into a title pill and round icon buttons. The 7‑icon tab bar floats at the bottom and the grid scrolls underneath. The selected tab also shows its name.

Bottom sheets instead of dialogs

Details, options, filters, sources, settings and quality pickers become grouped‑list sheets, like iOS. The main action is a solid white pill and amber is kept for selections.
